About NEW MOON Basic Education High School

NEW MOON Basic Education High School is a leading educational institution serving Rohingya students in the refugee camps of Bangladesh. Located in Camp-3, it has been providing education and hope to Rohingya children and youth since its establishment. The school is committed to quality education and helping students build a brighter future. It has become a respected and well-known institution within the Rohingya community. With dedicated teachers and determined students, NEW MOON continues to stand as a symbol of knowledge, resilience, and hope.
